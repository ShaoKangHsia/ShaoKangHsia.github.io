My research focuses on AI systems that can interpret physical-world tasks and turn multimodal evidence into useful assistance. I combine language and vision-language models with audio analysis, computer vision, retrieval, and AR interfaces.

## Featured projects

### SOUND: Sensemaking Over Unexpected Noise for Diagnosis of Everyday Machines

**Lead author · Submitted to CHI 2027**

SOUND uses machine sounds and video to help diagnose faults. The system identifies abnormal audio segments, extracts visual and acoustic cues, retrieves similar repair cases, and suggests likely faulty components. I built the multimodal pipeline and fine-tuned a BEATs classifier for seven types of machine noise.

### ARify: Leveraging Narrated Instructional Videos to Create Augmented Reality Tutorials for Procedural Tasks

**Co-lead author · CHI 2026** · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790715)

ARify helps non-programmers turn narrated instructional videos into spatially anchored AR tutorials. The workflow combines vision-language processing, object detection, segmentation, pose estimation, and hand tracking with a desktop editor for refining instructions and placement.

### agentAR: Creating Augmented Reality Applications with Tool-Augmented LLM-Based Autonomous Agents

**Co-lead author · UIST 2025** · [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3746059.3747676)

agentAR turns natural-language requests into AR applications using an LLM agent and a library of AR authoring tools. The work studies how an agent can plan and assemble applications using combinations of perception modules and AR elements.
