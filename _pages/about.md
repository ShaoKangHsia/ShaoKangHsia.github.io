I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at **Purdue University**, working in the C-Design Lab. My research explores how AI agents can understand physical-world tasks and provide useful, situated assistance. I build systems that bring together large language and vision-language models, computer vision, audio understanding, retrieval, and augmented reality.

My recent work includes tools for creating augmented reality tutorials from instructional video, AI-assisted AR authoring, and multimodal diagnosis of everyday machines. Previously, I studied electrical engineering at National Taiwan University and mechanical engineering at National Tsing Hua University.

[Explore my research](/research/) · [Read my publications](/publications/) · [Download my CV](/files/Shao-Kang_Hsia_CV.pdf)

## Research interests

- **AI agents for physical-world tasks:** planning and tool use grounded in multimodal observations and user feedback.
- **Multimodal learning:** combining video, audio, computer vision, and retrieved knowledge to understand real-world situations.
- **Augmented reality and human–AI interaction:** turning AI-generated guidance into spatial, actionable experiences.

## Selected work

**ARify** converts narrated instructional videos into spatially anchored AR tutorials. Published at CHI 2026. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3772318.3790715)

**agentAR** uses a tool-augmented language-model agent to create AR applications from natural-language instructions. Published at UIST 2025. [Paper](https://doi.org/10.1145/3746059.3747676)

**SOUND** combines machine audio and video to retrieve diagnostic cases and identify likely faulty components. Submitted to CHI 2027.
